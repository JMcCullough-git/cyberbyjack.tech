## About

I’m a passionate game artist and 3D modeler with extensive experience creating high-quality assets, characters, and environments for video games. As the Founder & Lead Artist at Darkfalls LLC, I not only create immersive game art but also lead a talented team of 3D artists, ensuring that every project meets the highest standards of visual fidelity and performance.  
  
🔹 What I Do:  
  
3D Modeling & Texturing (Maya, ZBrush, Substance Painter, Blender)  
Game Asset Creation for Unity & Unreal Engine  
Concept Art & Environment Design  
Leading a team of skilled modelers to deliver top-tier game-ready assets  
  
💡 Whether working independently or with my team, I specialize in bringing game worlds to life, ensuring that each asset enhances gameplay and storytelling. Our collaborative approach allows us to scale for larger projects, meet tight deadlines, and deliver industry-standard results.  
  
🚀 Open to New Opportunities:  
I’m always looking to collaborate with game studios, indie developers, and creative teams who need expert 3D artists to bring their visions to life. Feel free to connect with me!
